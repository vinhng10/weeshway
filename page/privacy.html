Information Collection and Use

We collect several different types of information for various purposes to provide and improve our Service to you.

Personal Data

While using our Service, we may ask you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information that can be used to contact or identify you ("Personal Data"). Personally identifiable information may include, but is not limited to:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Profile photo and date of birth (for age verification)
  • Dance style preferences, wish descriptions, and music preferences
  • Teacher profile information (qualifications, bio, class descriptions, venue details)
  • Payment information (billing name, payment method identifiers). Full card numbers are processed by Stripe and never stored by WeeshWay.
  • Reviews, ratings, photos, and videos you post on the Service
  • Messages exchanged through the Service, support requests, and dispute reports

We may use your Personal Data to contact you with booking confirmations, payment receipts, payout notifications, and other service-related communications. With your consent, we may also send marketing or promotional materials. You may opt out of receiving promotional communications at any time by using the unsubscribe link or by emailing at contact@weeshway.com.

Usage Data

We may also collect information that your device sends whenever you use our Service ("Usage Data").

This Usage Data may include information such as your device's Internet Protocol address (e.g. IP address), device type, operating system, app version, the features of our Service that you use, the time and date of your interactions, the time spent on those features, unique device identifiers and other diagnostic data.

Location Data

With your permission, we may collect and process information about your precise location using GPS. We use this information to show you nearby classes and improve geographic matching with Teachers. You can enable or disable location services at any time through your device settings. If precise location is disabled, we may still use your IP address to determine your approximate location.

Attendance Data

The Service provides an optional QR code check-in feature. When used, we collect a timestamp and confirmation of attendance at a class. This data is used for attendance verification and dispute resolution.

Recommendation System

WeeshWay uses an automated recommendation system to help Students discover relevant classes. The system uses mathematical representations (vector embeddings) to match your preferences with available classes based on dance style, music similarity, and location.

The recommendation system is a discovery aid only — it does not restrict your access to any class, and it does not make decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you. You can always browse, search, and book any available listing regardless of recommendations.

You may disable personalized recommendations at any time through App settings or by contacting us at contact@weeshway.com.
